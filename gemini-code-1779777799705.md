### Document: Admission Process & RSTNAT Exam
**Primary Tag:** `Admissions`, `RSTNAT`, `Eligibility`, `Scholarships`

**Admission Process (2026-27 Open):**
Admissions are straightforward. Students can apply online via the website or offline. Direct admission is possible for some courses through the management quota. 
* **UG Eligibility:** Passed 12th grade in any stream with at least 50% marks.
* **Engineering Eligibility:** 12th with Physics, Chemistry, and Maths (minimum 50%). Scores from JEE Main, COMEDK, CET, and TNEA are also accepted.
* **Required Documents:** 10th & 12th Marksheets, Transfer Certificate, Community Certificate (if applicable), Passport photos, Aadhar ID, Entrance scorecard (for engineering).
* **Payment:** Flexible installment options and education loan support are available. For exact application and tuition fees, contact admissions.

**RSTNAT (Raise Smart Scholarship Test & National Admission Test):**
* **Purpose:** Evaluates students for priority admission and merit-based scholarships.
* **Format:** Online computer-based MCQ test (50-100 questions, 60-90 minutes). Sections include Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, English, and General Awareness. 
* **Marking:** Usually features negative marking to reward accuracy.
* **Difficulty:** Moderately easy, focusing on basic concepts. Free mock tests are available.

* Options range from premium 2-sharing AC rooms to budget-friendly dormitories.
* Amenities include Wi-Fi, laundry, gym, TV room, study tables, and 24/7 water/electricity. Both veg and non-veg food (Sundays) are provided.

* For specific hostel room and mess fees, contact admissions.

**College Culture & Honest Realities:**
* **Professional Environment:** Raise Smart is not a "party college." It operates with corporate-level discipline to prepare students for the workplace.
* **Strict Rules:** 75% attendance is strictly mandatory to sit for exams. There is a professional dress code. 
* **Exam Failure Policy:** If a student fails, they have options: reappearance exams, supplementary exams, or carrying it as a backlog. Academic support is provided, but excessive backlogs will delay graduation and affect placements.
* **Study Abroad:** The degree is globally recognized. Students successfully apply to MS/MBA programs in the US, UK, Canada, etc., provided they maintain a strong GPA (8.5+) and clear exams like GRE/IELTS. The R-Smart AI certifications heavily boost foreign university applications.
