* **Outcome:** Skills are mandatory, measurable, and industry-aligned. RAALE turns learning into momentum and momentum into careers.

### Document: Real-World Experience Programs
**Primary Tag:** `Experience`, `Projects`, `Immersion`

**Build From Day One:**
Learning tech by creating it. At Raise Smart School of Technology, learning starts with building. From the first semester, students work on real-world projects, applications, and solutions. Example student tech stacks:
* Bennish Christopher M: Ruby on Rails, React and Typescript, Docker
* Abdul Rahman RSA: Ruby on Rails, React and Typescript, Docker
* Shaila Neelofar K: React and Typescript, MongoDB, FastAPI

**Field Exposure Programme (FEP):**
FEP is organized annually, offering structured visits conducted by corporate trainers. This program builds leadership mindset, discipline, and confidence through experiential challenges, guided activities, research centers, industries, and scientific institutions to connect classroom learning with real-world exposure.

**Global Immersion Programme:**
Learn beyond the classroom. Students interact with industry experts, global mentors, and technology leaders through workshops, guest sessions, and collaborative projects. This exposure helps students understand real industry challenges and global technology trends.
